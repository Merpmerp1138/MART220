For this assignment I wasn’t entirely sure what our objective was in creating an image with AI,
so I went with asking ChatGPT to create an image of a pineapple using p5.js. I chose to use
ChatGPT mainly because that’s the one I’m most familiar with. In the beginning it asked me a
few questions that surprised me. It asked if I wanted the image to be complex or if it should
be simple and stylized, and it also asked if I wanted a background color which it’s never asked 
before. I told it to use simple shapes, and a light blue background and in the end the resulting
image was pretty interesting. Definitely not the most amazing thing I’ve ever seen, but it was 
pretty good for being made out of p5.js shapes.
